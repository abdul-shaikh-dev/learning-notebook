How to install

On iPhone or iPad, open this site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (it may be under More). On Android or desktop, look in your browser menu for Install app or Add to Home screen.

If your browser does not offer installation, you can still save courses here and read them offline in this browser.

What comes with you?

Saved courses are available offline
Save a path before you leave your connection. Search can find every path, but lessons in unsaved paths need the internet.
Your progress stays on this device
Removing a download keeps reading progress. Installation does not sync devices. Back up your progress ↗ before clearing browser data.
Some activities still need a connection
External references, live APIs and online exercises require internet access. Downloaded code may also need its documented local tools or dependencies.
Storage belongs to your browser
The browser can clear saved files when space is low. Check the saved status before travelling. Downloads are managed separately in other browsers or devices.
