# Reduce load when the plan repeatedly fails: printable worksheet

Use this fictional case. You can answer aloud or make a short note; the space below is optional.

## Read

Repeated carry-over can mean the plan exceeds capacity, tasks are unclear or the environment changed. Look for structural explanations before adding stricter rules. An overload response removes, reduces, delays or shares work; simply tracking more precisely does not create time.

## Try before reading the answer

Four of six daily outcomes carry over on three days. Choose one change to try and explain what you would check afterward.

## Worked example

Check which outcomes are essential and which can be deferred. If two essential outcomes fit tomorrow, commit to those and agree to move the others. Compare actual durations. If essential obligations still exceed capacity, negotiate support or scope rather than extending the workday.

## Check your answer

- Reduce a named part of the next day's commitment.
- Check actual durations of the essential outcomes.
- Avoid solving capacity shortage through sleep reduction.
