# A sustainable personal system

Try a two-week routine and compare what happened with your plan.

## Deliverables

- Capture obligations and review capacity twice.
- Use a fictional conflicting request to practise agreeing on a feasible alternative.
- Remove one unnecessary tracking field.
- Report observed outcomes, maintenance cost and a next revision.

## Assessment

Score each criterion 0 (missing), 1 (partial) or 2 (supported). Revise any missing criterion before progressing. This is a learning self-assessment, not a professional certification.

- The routine has a minimum version for disrupted days.
- A conflicting request is resolved through explicit feasible alternatives.
- Tracking fields are justified by decisions and maintenance cost.
- Explain what else could account for the results, then choose a small follow-up trial.

## Worked synthetic approach

Keep one commitment list, a daily capacity check and a weekly review. For a 90-minute request with 30 minutes available, offer a partial result today or a full result Thursday. Record fewer misses alongside workload differences; retain useful decisions and revise unsupported claims.
