# A clear small project

Turn a synthetic workshop request into a scoped plan.

## Deliverables

- Define beneficiary, outcome and exclusions.
- Decompose into deliverables and next actions.
- Write completion criteria for three tasks.
- Map genuine dependencies and name owners.

## Assessment

Use these criteria to check your answer and choose what to revisit. A score is optional, and no completed worksheet is required to keep reading. This practice does not award a professional certification.

- Beneficiary, deliverables and excluded scope are explicit.
- Every deliverable has executable actions and an owner.
- Completion criteria describe checked artifacts.
- Dependency order separates required inputs from independent branches.

## Worked synthetic approach

For a 20-person beginner workshop, plan venue, exercise sheet and invitation. Confirm venue before publishing its address; drafting can run independently. Each item has an owner and checked completion state. Exclude video production from the initial scope.
