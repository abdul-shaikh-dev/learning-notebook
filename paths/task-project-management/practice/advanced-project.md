# Deliver and improve a project

Close a project using acceptance and improvement evidence.

## Deliverables

- Calculate age, cycle time and throughput with explicit boundaries.
- Perform verification and a beneficiary validation task.
- Review one recurring workflow bottleneck.
- Record accepted outputs, unresolved obligations and a follow-up owner.

## Assessment

Use these criteria to check your answer and choose what to revisit. A score is optional, and no completed worksheet is required to keep reading. This practice does not award a professional certification.

- Age, cycle time and throughput use consistent boundaries.
- Acceptance includes both specification checks and intended-use evidence.
- The improvement trial addresses an observed workflow constraint.
- Closure links accepted outputs and transfers remaining obligations.

## Worked synthetic approach

A Monday-to-Thursday card has three elapsed days of cycle time; an unfinished Tuesday card is three days old Friday. A beginner struggles to find the first exercise step despite a complete checklist, so revise and retest. Close with accepted links and a named owner for the access issue; trial scheduled reviews for recurring wait time.
