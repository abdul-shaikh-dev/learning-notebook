Estimated routine service work totals 12 hours: Monday 2, Tuesday 3, Wednesday 2, Thursday 3, Friday 2. Remaining daytime capacity is Monday 3, Tuesday 2, Wednesday 1, Thursday 2, Friday 3: 11 hours altogether. Exact clock times must be checked locally before placing blocks; the table is a day-level budget.

The agreed event pack is due Friday 16:00 and takes an estimated eight hours: venue check and cover two, drafting two, names reconciliation two, final check and delivery two. Lee supplies names by Tuesday noon; request Monday and follow up Tuesday if missing. A four-hour noticeboard redesign is an optional idea. Sam has a Thursday appointment already included among fixed duties. Wednesday evening is a family care commitment. Two optional short learning occasions fit Tuesday lunch's last 12 minutes if lunch remains restorative, and Thursday evening eight minutes if genuinely available; neither is counted as working-hour capacity. Reserve three of the 11 work hours for uncertainty. These are scenario choices, not universal planning percentages.

## Exact tasks

1. Capture accepted commitments, optional ideas and waiting items. State the Friday result and the names dependency.
2. Reconcile 35 minus 12 minus 12 and the day-level capacities. Explain why 11 is an estimate, not guaranteed focus.
3. Place the eight event hours by day and leave three hours unscheduled. Respect Wednesday care and the Thursday fixed appointment.
4. Name what is deferred and draft a late-names fallback requiring an event owner's decision rather than invented data.
5. Add one learning target and feasible check, then score the rubric. Use week-planner.md.
