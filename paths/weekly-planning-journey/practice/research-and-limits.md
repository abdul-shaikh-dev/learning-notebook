# Weekly planning: research and limits

Reviewed 2026-09-30. This is an original fictional planning journey, not a validated intervention or a guarantee of productivity. The exact hour budgets, number of outcomes, reserve and experiment dates are scenario design choices.

- [Harkin et al. 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26479070/) reviews experimental monitoring interventions. Our light log prompts comparison, but its rubric measures completeness of scenario reasoning, not psychological health or personal improvement.
- [Gollwitzer 1999](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.54.7.493) informs cue-action planning. A cue cannot create capacity, authorise a deadline change or remove a care responsibility.
- [Roediger & Karpicke 2006](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x/) informs the small closed-source learning check. The original prose-learning outcome is narrower than every work skill.
- [Hattie & Timperley 2007](https://doi.org/10.3102/003465430298487) informs specific feedback and repair. The role-play communication is a teaching adaptation, not a tested script for every workplace.

Consult actual commitments and authorised decision owners when adapting a plan. Elapsed spare time cannot be moved into the future. Estimates and observed hours are different. Keep proposed experiments pending until they occur. The examples do not diagnose fatigue, prescribe treatment or rank personal worth by completed tasks. You can choose a smaller plan, a pause or a different accessible format when the week requires it.
