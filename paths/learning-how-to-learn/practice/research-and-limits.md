# Research register and limits

Reviewed 2026-09-30. Primary research and reviews inform the techniques; dates, counts and fictional policies are original teaching examples. Publisher links may offer abstracts rather than free full text. None is required to complete the offline worksheets.

- [Dunlosky et al. 2013](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1529100612453266): compares ten learning techniques and their conditions. A technique's value depends on the task and learner; these lessons do not promise a universal best method.
- [Roediger & Karpicke 2006](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x/): studied prose-learning and later recall. The practical retrieval loop here includes correction and new fictional cases; it is not a replication of their experiments.
- [Cepeda et al. 2006](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/3rr6q10c): synthesises distributed verbal-recall practice. Our example weekdays are chosen for feasibility, not a validated optimal interval formula.
- [Rohrer & Taylor 2007](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11251-007-9015-8): mathematics practice experiments inform mixing related cases. This is not evidence that frequent unrelated task switching improves work.
- [Hattie & Timperley 2007](https://doi.org/10.3102/003465430298487): feedback review; the actionable comments in the kits are adaptations. Feedback can be useful or unhelpful depending on its information and use.
- [Butler 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20804289/): prose retention and inference transfer experiments. Changed fictional policy cases do not establish every form of real-world transfer.
- [Gollwitzer 1999](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.54.7.493): cue-action plans. A plan cannot remove a care duty or create spare time.
- [Harkin et al. 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26479070/): experimental progress-monitoring synthesis. Our logs support reflection; they are not a validated diagnostic tool.

Use access-friendly formats and task-appropriate practice. This course does not assign fixed learning-style identities. It does not guarantee perfect memory, diagnose learning difficulty, prescribe treatment or equate a short quiz with professional competence. Consult real authoritative rules before acting outside the fictional exercises.
