# Intermediate kit: a flexible practice plan

Use the supplied policy or a safe topic with a checkable source. The full topic does not need to be memorised if real use allows a reference; define your target accordingly.

## Exact tasks

1. Choose the target and source. Bring forward the foundation baseline and one error. Classify it as missing knowledge, case reading, method selection or a tentative slip; say why.
2. Prepare six related cases in varied order and a separate key. State the feature that distinguishes each category. Do not label the cases with their answer category.
3. Identify two or three actual available windows across two weeks. Assign each a purpose: explanation, retrieval with correction, mixed cases, or delayed check.
4. Write a normal cue and a fallback that respects work, care and rest. Specify what you will omit if a window disappears.
5. Before a five-question check, predict a count. After checking, compare predicted and actual counts and inspect confidently wrong answers. Record support.
6. Complete the available occasions and record them. Add one feedback statement naming a specific gap and repair, then try a fresh case. Mark future occasions pending.

## Worked plan — synthetic

Target: route planned short, planned long and urgent same-day leave requests with a reason. Error: knows the ordinary duration rule but omitted urgency; tentatively method selection. Repair: explicitly check urgency before duration.

Mixed cases and key: planned one day → coordinator; urgent one day today → manager; planned three days → manager; planned one day next month → coordinator; planned two days → manager; urgent half day today → manager. The reasons follow urgency first, then planned duration. Merely alternating words without choosing rules would not test selection.

Available windows: Tuesday week one 12 minutes for explanation and first attempt; Thursday eight minutes for mixed cases and correction; Monday week two ten minutes for new cases after a gap. Cue: after lunch, open the prepared questions. Fallback: if Thursday is unavailable, remove that occasion, retain Monday and narrow its check. Wednesday care evening is not a catch-up slot.

Prediction Thursday: 4/5 correct. Actual: 3/5. Confidently wrong urgent case: coordinator because one day. Feedback: you used duration before checking the urgent override; state urgency first and try a new half-day urgent case. Fresh immediate answer: manager because urgent today. This immediate repair is not itself delayed retention evidence.

Disruption alternative: Thursday cancelled. Log Tuesday done, Thursday cancelled, Monday pending. Do not copy the 3/5 sample into your actual record. The fallback is still sensible, but a completed two-occasion project requires the later attempt to occur.

## Check your attempt

Check whether each practice session has a purpose, the mixed cases make you choose a rule, and the fallback fits your available time. Compare your prediction with your actual answers and corrections. Scoring is optional. A schedule shows what you plan to do; only a completed attempt shows what you could answer.
