# Sources and limits

Reviewed 2026-09-30. References are primary research where empirical claims are made and first-party public/university guidance for practical techniques. Exercises and cases are original educational applications, not validated instruments.

## University of Michigan Ombuds: Collaborative Conflict Resolution

https://ombuds.umich.edu/article/principles-collaborative-conflict-resolution

Section: Multiple perspectives and collaborative resolution. University practical guidance, not experimental proof that every disagreement can be resolved collaboratively.

## Oregon State University Ombuds: Giving and Receiving Feedback

https://ombuds.oregonstate.edu/sites/ombuds.oregonstate.edu/files/2026-01/OMBUDS%20Handouts_Feedback.pdf

Section: Giving and receiving feedback tip sheet. University educational handout; used for specific, respectful feedback, not a clinical intervention.

## Harvard Ombuds Office: resources and active listening

https://hms.harvard.edu/sites/default/files/assets/Sites/Ombuds/files/HMS.HSDM_.HSPH_.OmbudsOffice.ActiveListeningTechniques.pdf

Section: Active Listening Techniques handout listing. University practice guidance; techniques encourage understanding, not agreement or a guaranteed response.

## University of Michigan Ombuds: Preparing for a Challenging Conversation

https://ombuds.umich.edu/article/preparing-challenging-conversation

Section: Preparation, non-blaming language and follow-up. University conflict guidance for voluntary everyday conversations; power differences and formal reporting needs may limit suitability.

## NHS Every Mind Matters: Reframing unhelpful thoughts

https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/reframing-unhelpful-thoughts/

Section: Catch, check, change; considering evidence and alternatives. Public guidance adapted only for everyday reflection on fictional low-stakes situations; no diagnosis, therapy protocol or treatment promise.

Do not infer causation from a personal before/after comparison. No universal habit deadline, guaranteed communication outcome or fixed willpower resource is assumed. Adapt for available time, disability, care responsibilities, culture and power differences.
