# Financial foundations progression review

Reviewed 2 October 2026. Read all 24 core lessons: starter IDs 1–6 and curriculum IDs 1–18. Also reviewed the eight supporting foundation explainers, advanced extensions, six exercise groups and six revision exercises, seven lab guides, the six-part month-end case, eight-part trade journey, and all three downloadable practice-task briefs. Accounting and regulatory requirements were not changed.

## Changes

- Curriculum lesson 1 now walks through dividing a per-100 quote by 100 before multiplying by face, then adding accrued interest. Its exercise can be attempted without guessing the scale from the worked totals.
- Curriculum lesson 3 starts with one supplied discount factor and then two fixed payments before introducing curves, swaps and sensitivities. New arithmetic is 100 × 0.95 + 50 × 0.90 = 140.
- Curriculum lesson 15 defines the formula symbols before use and distinguishes prudent value from the earlier meaning of PV as present value. The existing sourced methods and conditions remain unchanged.
- Curriculum lesson 18 now offers a changed-position case before the worked answer. Halving face gives a signed price difference of −15,000; the separate deduction and prudential assumptions cannot be scaled without a revised contract.
- Supporting explainer `daily-pnl` now appears with lessons 16 and 17. Previously it appeared in lesson 1 and lesson 6, bringing multi-date FX attribution and cost-allocation methods into the first instrument introduction.
- Supporting explainer `swaps-options` now gives a short staged handoff to the optional swap workbook instead of appending the entire forecast/discount attribution result to the beginner instrument primer.
- Resource task `practice` starts with bond rows after source-quality preparation, then directs swap/option interpretation to lesson 3. Resource task `swap-repricing` no longer requires the combined daily-P&L/FX workbook; the fixed-cash-flow preparation in lesson 3 is enough for the supplied same-time comparison.

## Content retained after review

The starter sequence already moves from trades to positions, groupings, units, value/cash distinctions and control evidence. Its changed-number quiz cases are appropriate preparation for the main lessons, so it was preserved.

The hierarchy sequence already distinguishes observability from significance, then applies those ideas to conditional scenarios. It was preserved rather than adding a new generic quiz. The prudent-valuation sequence keeps supplied diagnostic arithmetic separate from method-specific regulatory totals. The month-end case and revision exercises already mix units, evidence and accounting/prudential distinctions, so the targeted new variation adds transfer without replacing them.
