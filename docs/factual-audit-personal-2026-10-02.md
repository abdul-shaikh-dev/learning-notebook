# Factual audit: personal learning paths

Review date: 2026-10-02. Baseline supplied for this audit: `4efaedb`.

Scope: all 117 lesson records in the six paths below, including takeaways, explanatory sections, worked cases, exercise prompts/solutions/checks and quiz choices/explanations. The reading combines the preceding editorial pass with this factual pass; previously truncated sections were reopened in smaller selections. Every lesson has a row below. Public worksheets were consulted for scenario context and corrected mirrors, but this is not a line-by-line factual audit of every downloadable file or the studio runtime.

Status means checked against the stated fictional facts and the cited source scope, not scientifically validated as a complete course. Most planning and communication advice is a practical recommendation rather than an empirical efficacy claim. Source abstracts are identified where full papers were not read. No human learning trial, raw-study-data replication, clinical assessment or institutional-policy certification was performed.

Three confirmed corrections: overload answer assumed unspecified tasks were optional; Wen review invented a lighter timetable; a proposed practice schedule was described as already supplying delayed evidence. Arithmetic checks found no incorrect stated numeric result in these 117 lessons. Quiz option indices were checked for validity and answers against their cases.

## Source access and scope

- **TM**: [TM](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0245066). Full article, discussion and limitations. Associations do not validate the exact local schedules.
- **NASA**: [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-0-crosscutting-technical-management/). Official handbook, technical planning/risk/decision sections. Normative engineering guidance adapted to everyday cases.
- **DESIGN**: [DESIGN](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/4-0-system-design-processes/). Official handbook, stakeholder expectations and requirements.
- **VERIFY**: [VERIFY](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/5-0-product-realization/). Official handbook, verification and validation distinctions.
- **KANBAN**: [KANBAN](https://kanbanguides.org/the-kanban-guide/2025.5/). May 2025 definition of workflow and four flow metrics. Normative method, not causal trial.
- **SCRUM**: [SCRUM](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html). November 2020 guide and End Note; partial practices do not constitute Scrum.
- **SLEEP**: [SLEEP](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-deprivation/healthy-sleep-habits). Official health guidance. No individual diagnosis or medical sleep prescription audited here.
- **INTERRUPT**: [INTERRUPT](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/CHI_2007_Iqbal_Horvitz-1.pdf). Original study PDF; field observations and design implications. No universal interruption cost or tested return-note format.
- **FALLACY**: [FALLACY](https://bear.warrington.ufl.edu/brenner/mar7588/Papers/buehler-et-al-1994.pdf). Original 1994 paper, including general discussion of optimism versus absolute prediction accuracy.
- **HABIT**: [HABIT](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10144814/1/23311908.2022.pdf). Gardner/Rebar/Lally 2022 original methodological paper. Habit measurement differs from counting behaviour.
- **LALLY**: [LALLY](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ejsp.674). Publisher abstract read: model fits,95% asymptote range 18-254 days, single missed opportunity. Full raw-data reanalysis not performed.
- **IFTHEN**: [IFTHEN](https://sparq.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj19021/files/media/file/gollwitzer_brandstatter_1997_-_implementation_intentions_effective_goal_pursuit.pdf). Original 1997 correlational and experimental studies; situation-action definition.1999 review DOI/download failed, so no claim of reading that full review.
- **LEARN**: [LEARN](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1529100612453266). Original 2013 review, sections 2,8,9,10 and scope qualifications. Not evidence for each bespoke worksheet.
- **RETRIEVE**: [RETRIEVE](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x/). Original publisher abstract: prose recall, immediate and delayed checks, no-feedback study conditions.
- **SPACE**: [SPACE](https://escholarship.org/content/qt3rr6q10c/qt3rr6q10c.pdf). Original manuscript; interval/retention-horizon analyses. Repository notes manuscript may differ from final publication.
- **MIX**: [MIX](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11251-007-9015-8). Publisher abstract read: two college mathematics experiments; does not establish universal interleaving benefit.
- **TRANSFER**: [TRANSFER](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20804289/). Original article abstract indexed by PubMed; prose-learning inference questions, limited transfer scope.
- **FEEDBACK**: [FEEDBACK](https://educacion.udd.cl/files/2018/04/The-Power-of-Feedback.pdf). Original 2007 review PDF; task/process/self-regulation versus personal feedback and next-step model.
- **STYLES**: [STYLES](https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2016/07/Pashler_McDaniel_Rohrer_Bjork_2009_PSPI.pdf). Original review summary via university-hosted indexed PDF; preferences do not establish matching efficacy.
- **MONITOR**: [MONITOR](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/91437/8/3_PDFsam_Does%20monitoring%20goal.pdf). Original author manuscript available through university repository; experimental intervention synthesis, not validation of Sam worksheet. PubMed/direct publisher fetch failed.
- **REFLECT**: [REFLECT](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/reframing-unhelpful-thoughts/). Official guidance on checking evidence and alternatives; ordinary course exercise is not clinical assessment.
- **LISTEN**: [LISTEN](https://hms.harvard.edu/sites/default/files/assets/Sites/Ombuds/files/HMS.HSDM_.HSPH_.OmbudsOffice.ActiveListeningTechniques.pdf). Primary institutional handout; encouraging/clarifying/restating/validating. Guidance, not controlled efficacy evidence.
- **CONFLICT**: [CONFLICT](https://ombuds.umich.edu/article/principles-collaborative-conflict-resolution). Primary institutional guidance: roles, context, consultation and limits of conflict resolution.
- **CONVERSATION**: [CONVERSATION](https://ombuds.umich.edu/article/preparing-challenging-conversation). Primary institutional guidance: timing, facts, non-blaming wording, summary and follow-up.
- **GIVE**: [GIVE](https://ombuds.oregonstate.edu/sites/ombuds.oregonstate.edu/files/2026-01/OMBUDS%20Handouts_Feedback.pdf). Primary institutional feedback handout; specific events, impact, alternative perspectives. Not a guaranteed response.
