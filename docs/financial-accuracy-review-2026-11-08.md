## Corrections made

1. Lesson 16 (Go deeper) and lesson 21 (Common trap) said a large holding does not "automatically" justify a blockage
   discount and warned against a "blanket" concentration discount. IFRS 13 paragraphs 69 and 80 prohibit a blockage
   discount to a Level 1 price outright. Both now say so.
2. Lesson 29 (Go deeper) said the EU calculation is "at least quarterly". Article 1 of the RTS says quarterly; the
   lesson now matches.
